# Yunjiang Brand Copy Skill

Use this skill for Yunjiang Lounge brand copy.

## Brand Position

Yunjiang Lounge is a city business lounge and enterprise gift-liquor solution based on Guizhou sauce-aroma liquor production resources.

It is not:

- A cheap baijiu shop
- A scattered liquor stall
- A franchise advertisement page
- A loud investment招商 website
- A mass-market supermarket liquor shelf

It should feel:

- Premium
- Warm
- Trustworthy
- Business-oriented
- Calm
- Human
- Refined
- Suitable for enterprise reception and gifting

## Preferred Copy Style

Use Chinese copy that is:

- Clear
- Restrained
- Warm
- Slightly poetic but not fake
- Business-friendly
- Easy to understand
- Not overly academic
- Not too internet-slang heavy

Good directions:

- 让每一次商务往来，都值得被记住
- 从产区酒厂，到城市会客厅
- 为企业礼赠、客户接待与商务品鉴，提供更稳妥的酱酒方案
- 先理解场景，再推荐礼酒
- 不只是一瓶酒，而是一段被认真对待的关系

## Avoid

Avoid phrases like:

- 财富风口
- 火爆招商
- 千亿赛道
- 诚邀加盟
- 爆款引流
- 一夜出圈
- 高端大气上档次
- 源头厂家直供，全网最低价
- 送礼就送云酱

Avoid excessive claims:

- national-level claims without evidence
- guaranteed business conversion
- exaggerated Maotai comparisons
- unrealistic luxury language

## Writing Rules

When rewriting copy:

1. Keep the brand calm and premium.
2. Preserve factual accuracy.
3. Avoid empty slogans.
4. Make CTA text clear and useful.
5. Keep website copy concise.
6. Keep business-plan copy more formal if requested.
7. Do not invent unsupported production data.
